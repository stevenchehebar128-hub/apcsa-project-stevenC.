## What it does
The app allows users to rate movies, read reviews from other users, and see average ratings to help them decide which movies are worth watching. Users can search for movies and compare ratings to find movies that match their interests.

## Features
Movie Search: Search for movies by title or genre.
Movie Ratings: Give movies a rating from 1 to 10.
User Reviews: Write and read reviews to see what other people think about a movie.


Recommendations: Suggest movies based on users' favorite genres and ratings.
